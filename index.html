Rules and sketch space · Draft v0.3

Every request a traveller might type, and the cards we answer it with.

PDG Flights builds its screen per request. The AI reads what the traveller asked, picks one result, and builds it from a fixed kit of cards. This page maps what travellers ask (scenarios) to what we build answers from (components). Then you can test any request below.

    Part 2 · Components

    What we build answers from

    Four layers, each built only from the one below. The AI picks the result, chooses and fills the cards, and adds or leaves out blocks. Designers own the atoms and decide which blocks each card can hold.

    Layer 1AtomsButton, input, badge, pill, chip, icon, tokens
    Layer 2BlocksSmall groups of atoms inside a card
    Layer 3 · AI fillsCardsOne job each
    Layer 4 · AI picksResultsA fixed recipe of cards for one kind of ask

    Cards we have

    12 cards. Open one to see it and its four rules.

    Cards to build

    3 cards cover every proposed scenario in scope. Sketches only.

    Blocks

    Add a block only if it makes the card clearer or the decision quicker. If it wouldn't change what the traveller picks, leave it out.
